Dinner Truce

Privacy Policy · Last updated: February 9, 2026

The short version: Dinner Truce has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Your recipes and decisions are stored only on your phone. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you deliberately share or export it.

What we collect

Nothing. Dinner Truce has no servers and no accounts, and we receive no data from your use of the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Your recipe library, favourites, decision history, shopping lists, and collections — stored locally in the app's private storage.
  • Android's optional device backup may copy app data to your Google account's backup, per your device settings. The built-in recipe catalog is excluded from platform backup by design; exports are always a deliberate, confirmed action by you.
  • One-tap export creates a JSON file — you choose where it goes.

When data leaves your phone (only because you chose it)

  • Sharing a recipe or collection uses the Android share sheet: it sends exactly what you selected to the destination you picked.
  • Voice recipe entry uses Android's built-in speech recognition. Audio is handled by Android's speech service under its own terms; the app itself works only with the recognized text.
  • Importing recipes from links or photos — the app fetches only the content you ask it to import.
  • Optional AI features (off by default) — if you enable AI autofill in Settings, the recipe text you submit is sent to the configured AI service to draft a structured entry, and the result is clearly labeled. Turning the switch off stops this.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only while you dictate a recipe.
  • Notifications — only for the optional dinner reminder you set.
  • Internet — for imports you request and, if you enable it, AI autofill.

Children

Dinner Truce is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future feature changes what data is processed — for example new optional services — we will update this page and the store listing before that version ships.
